LabelIQ
Demo Prototype

Scan a packaged product label and get a Legal Metrology compliance verdict in seconds, entirely on the phone.

iQOO Hackathon 2026 / Hyderabad City Battle
Track: FinTech and Commerce
1

Capture

Photograph the panels that carry the declarations. Add as many shots as the pack needs, since the MRP, net quantity and consumer care details are often printed on different faces.

2

Extract

On-device OCR turns the label into structured text. Nothing leaves the device.

OCR runs on Tesseract.js (WebAssembly) inside the browser. The engine file has to download once on a first run, so if the device is offline use Load Sample Label or type the label text in directly.

3

Validate

A rule engine checks the extracted text against the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011.

4

Compliance Report

0%
Compliance Score

Product Digital Twin

Loose label text is normalised into a key-value record, so declared values can be checked against each other.

Who It Is Built For

Phone app
Shoppers
Check the expiry, confirm the MRP before paying, and catch packs where the printed unit price does not add up.
Field mode
Legal Metrology inspectors
Work through a warehouse rack quickly, with a timestamped photo and report kept as evidence for each pack.
Studio review
Brands and packaging teams
Run artwork through the same rules before it goes to print, instead of finding the gap after a recall.
